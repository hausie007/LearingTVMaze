Fun safe maze! Teaches kids letters, numbers and languages directly on TV

Turn your TV into a safe and playful learning environment for your little ones!

The Learning Maze game is an interactive educational maze created specifically for preschool children. It is designed to be easily controlled using only the directional arrows on your TV remote.

While finding the right path through the maze, children encounter various letters or numbers. The game cleverly connects visual cues with spoken words, allowing kids to naturally and stress-free learn the basics of the alphabet and counting. So that learning isn't boring, they can choose from several cheerful and colorful visual themes, such as a car race, ducks on a pond, a robber and a police officer, and more.

🌍 Ideal for first words and foreign languages
The game is fully localized and includes voice output (TTS) for 20+ world languages! It is thus a great tool not only for the mother tongue but also for a playful introduction to a foreign language.
Supported languages: English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, German, Turkish, Vietnamese, Italian, Polish, Dutch, Romanian, Ukrainian, Swedish, Czech, Hungarian, Greek, Hebrew, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Slovak.

Why kids and parents will love the game:

🎮 Made for TV: Large graphics and absolutely simple control with a standard TV remote. Helps teach small children fine motor skills and basic TV control using the arrows on the remote.

🔤 Learning through play: Fun recognition of numbers and letters using both sound and image.

🗣️ Multilingualism: A great tool for bilingual families or early language learning.

🛡️ 100% Safe for kids: The game contains ABSOLUTELY NO ADS or hidden purchases.

🔒 Maximum privacy: The game works fully offline and collects no personal data.
